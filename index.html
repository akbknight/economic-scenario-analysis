Model Notes

  • Unemployment projection uses Okun-style approximation: each 1pp rise in the fed rate above 2.5% adds ~0.15pp to unemployment over 12 months.
  • CPI projection applies a 30% pass-through persistence and an 8bp rate drag per 100bp of fed rate.
  • Recession probability uses a logistic function calibrated to historical rate-unemployment gaps.
