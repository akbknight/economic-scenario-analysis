Econometric Model Framework & Illustrative Calibration

01 · Problem Statement

Macroeconomic stress scenarios require sensitivity exploration across policy rates, inflation persistence, and labor slack rather than isolated static cases.

02 · Okun & Policy Transmission

Calibrated baseline applies stylized heuristics from macro literature (Taylor/Okun rules). Empirical prototype propagates OLS parameter uncertainty from historical FRED benchmark data (2000–2024).

03 · Landmark Snapshots & Gap Scope

The 16 historical years (2000–2024) are selected landmark benchmark snapshots illustrating cyclical extremes, not a continuous annual estimation panel. Gaps of 2 to 5 years are documented and excluded from uniform 1-year lag specifications.

04 · Confidence vs. Prediction Intervals

Reported uncertainty bands represent conditional-mean confidence intervals (±1.96·SE of expected response). Forecast prediction intervals for individual years are substantially wider due to irreducible residual variance.
